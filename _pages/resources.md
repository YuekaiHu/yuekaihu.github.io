This page provides datasets, visualization tools, and reproducible resources developed for monitoring China's coastal ecosystems. Each resource will include dataset documentation, spatial and temporal coverage, data-access links, recommended citations, and an interactive Google Earth Engine explorer where available.

      <h2 style="margin-top:0;">China Coastal Salt Marsh Dataset</h2>
      <p>A national-scale dataset describing the distribution and dominant vegetation communities of coastal salt marshes in China. It provides a consistent baseline for studying salt-marsh distribution, invasive <em>Spartina alterniflora</em>, native vegetation communities, habitat conservation, and coastal restoration.</p>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Coverage:</strong> Coastal mainland China</li>
        <li><strong>Reference year:</strong> 2019</li>
        <li><strong>Spatial resolution:</strong> 10 m</li>
        <li><strong>Source:</strong> Sentinel-1 SAR time series</li>
        <li><strong>Classes:</strong> <em>Spartina alterniflora</em>, <em>Phragmites australis</em>, <em>Suaeda</em> spp., and <em>Scirpus</em> spp.</li>
        <li><strong>Overall accuracy:</strong> 87.3%</li>
        <li><strong>Status:</strong> Preparing for release</li>
      </ul>

      <h2 style="margin-top:0;">China Coastal Tidal Flat Dataset</h2>
      <p>A multi-temporal dataset for monitoring the distribution and evolution of tidal flats along China's coast. It is designed to support research on tidal-flat loss and expansion, reclamation, salt-marsh encroachment, coastal erosion, habitat change, and regional restoration.</p>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Coverage:</strong> Coastal mainland China</li>
        <li><strong>Temporal coverage:</strong> 1990–2020</li>
        <li><strong>Temporal interval:</strong> Approximately three years</li>
        <li><strong>Source:</strong> Landsat 5 and Landsat 8</li>
        <li><strong>Reported accuracy:</strong> Above 95%</li>
        <li><strong>Products:</strong> Tidal-flat extent maps and change layers</li>
        <li><strong>Status:</strong> In preparation</li>
      </ul>
