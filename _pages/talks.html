Conference Presentations

Mapping Tidal Flat Changes and Determining Drivers in China's Coastal Zones

Yuekai Hu, Lin Yuan, Huifang Fan, Yuwen Pang, Yao Li, Qiannan Ding, Juntian Liu, Bo Tian, and Yunxuan Zhou

EGU General Assembly 2024, Vienna, Austria, 14–19 April 2024.

This presentation introduced an efficient and reproducible framework for national-scale tidal-flat mapping using long-term Landsat observations, automated low-tide image selection, and knowledge-guided extraction.
